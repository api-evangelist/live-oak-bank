Competing against big brands with bigger teams, bigger budgets and bigger software can feel like an uphill battle for small business owners who juggle marketing alongside everything else it takes to run a company. The good news is that AI marketing tools are leveling the playing field, giving leaner teams an easier way to create content faster, keep messaging consistent, personalize outreach and stretch limited marketing dollars further. Nearly half of small businesses use AI for marketing , and that number is only expected to grow.
