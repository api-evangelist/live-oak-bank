Every small business owner has felt it and heard it: the constant reminder that AI is essential, paired with the fear of wasting time and money on tools that don’t fit the business. The reality is a little more practical and simpler than the reminders suggest. AI tools can support the everyday work of running a business, including repeat tasks, customer communication, marketing, productivity, financial organization and decision-making.
